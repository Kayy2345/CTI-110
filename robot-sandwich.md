# Robot Instructions For a Turkey Sandwich

## Assumptions

* The robot is standing in the kitchen empty-handed.
* There is bread in the pantry and turkey, cheese, mayonnaise, and mustard in the fridge.
* A plate and a knife are available in the kitchen cabinets.

## Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Walk over to the kitchen cabinet, open it, and grab a plate.
2. Put the plate down flat on the counter.
3. Walk over to the kitchen drawer, open it and pick up a butter knife by the handle.
4. Set the butter knife on the counter next to the plate.
5. Walk over to the pantry, grab the bag of bread, and place it on the counter next to the plate.
6. Untwist the twist-tie on the bread bag and set the twist-tie on the counter.
7. Open the bread bag, take out two slices of bread, and lay them flat on the plate side-by-side.
8. Name the first slice of bread "Slice 1" and the second slice "Slice 2."
9. Walk over to the refrigerator, open it, and take out the turkey, cheese, mayonnaise, and mustard.
10. Set all of those food items down on the counter next to the plate.
11. Open the mayonnaise jar lid and set it down on the counter.
12. Use the butter knife by the handle to scoop some mayonnaise out of the jar.
13. Spread the mayonnaise across Slice 1.
14. Pick up the mustard bottle and squeeze a little bit of mustard directly onto Slice 1.
15. Put the mustard bottle down on the counter.
16. Put the butter knife down on the counter. 
17. Open the packages of turkey and cheese.
18. Take out a slice of turkey and place it flat on Slice 2.
19. Take out a slice of cheese and lay it flat on Slice 2.
20. Pick up Slice 1.
21. Flip it over and press it down on Slice 2.
22. Pick up the butter knife by the handle and slice down through the middle to cut the sandwich in half.

## Done When

The robot is done when the turkey and cheese are inside the bread, the sandwich is cut in half on the plate, and the robot stands still.
